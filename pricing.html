Questions

Before you ask

Does any of our data reach Panopticon?

No. Real values stay on the employee’s laptop. The AI tool gets the masked prompt (or nothing, if blocked), your own server gets a masked event with the data type, a masked snippet and a one-way hash, and the backend runs on your servers.

What does the free audit change on our laptops?

Nothing is blocked. The audit runs in inventory-only mode for 30 days on up to 50 laptops and ends with a Shadow-AI Risk Report mapped to GDPR, the AI Act and NIS2.

Is the pilot fee lost if we buy?

No. The paid pilot (€3,000–5,000 for up to 200 users) is credited in full when you convert to a subscription.

Which plan includes OCR on screenshots and scans?

Business and Sovereign. Protect checks typed and pasted text in browser and desktop AI tools.

Are you SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certified?

Not yet. The dashboard and logs are self-hosted, so they stay under your own security controls. Sovereign includes a DPIA pack.
