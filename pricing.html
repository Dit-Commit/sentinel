Questions

Before you ask

Does any of our data reach Panopticon?

No. Real values stay on the employee’s computer. The AI tool gets the masked prompt (or nothing, if blocked), your own server gets a masked event with the data type, a masked snippet and a one-way hash, and the backend runs on your servers.

What does the Discovery audit change on our devices?

Nothing is blocked. The Discovery audit runs in inventory-only mode for 30 days for up to 200 users and ends with a Shadow-AI Risk Report mapped to GDPR, the AI Act and NIS2.

What happens when the Discovery audit ends?

On day 31 access ends. The data collected during the audit stays with you: everything runs on your own servers, so it is yours to keep or delete.

Is the pilot fee lost if we buy?

No. The paid pilot (€3,000–5,000 for up to 200 users) is credited in full when you convert to a subscription.

Which plan includes OCR on screenshots and scans?

Business and Sovereign. Protect checks typed and pasted text in browser and desktop AI tools.

Are you SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certified?

Not yet. The dashboard and logs are self-hosted, so they stay under your own security controls. A DPIA pack for Sovereign is planned Roadmap.
