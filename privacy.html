Privacy

How this website handles your data

Short version: no cookies, no tracking, no analytics, no third-party requests. The host keeps a security log of visits, and we see your email only if you write to us.

Last updated 21 November 2026. This notice covers this website. Sentinel itself runs on the customer’s own servers: it sends nothing to us.

Who is responsible

The Panopticon Sentinel team (Athens, Greece), which builds Sentinel. Contact for anything in this notice: marios.fragakis@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Nothing while you browse. The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, tracking pixels, advertising or session recording. Fonts, images, the demo video and its music are served from this site itself, so your browser contacts no other company while you read it.
  • Your theme choice. If you switch between light and dark, your browser remembers the choice in its own local storage (one entry named theme). It never leaves your device, and you can clear it in your browser settings at any time.
  • Emails you send us. The “Talk to us” and audit buttons open your own email program. If you write, we receive what you send (usually your name, email address, company and message) and use it only to reply and to discuss Sentinel with you. Legal basis: steps you ask us to take before a possible contract, and our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and (f)). We keep these emails as long as the conversation is useful, then delete them, at the latest 24 months after the last contact, unless the law requires otherwise.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. When you visit, GitHub logs your IP address for security purposes, whether or not you have a GitHub account; we do not receive or use these logs. GitHub may process this data outside the EU. Details are in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, restrict or object to its use, and receive it in a portable format. Write to the address above; we answer within one month. You can also complain to a data protection authority; in Greece that is the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (dpa.gr).

Changes

If what the site does with data changes, for example if we add a contact form, we will update this page and its date first.
